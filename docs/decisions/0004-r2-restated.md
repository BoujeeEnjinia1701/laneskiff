# 0004: R2 restated

- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Status:** accepted. Decided by Amish on 2026-10-04: "For round 3, I agree with all your proposed recommendations". For LaneSkiff this accepts option A of open item 1 in LSK-DEC-001 (portfolio item 6A).

## Context

After the five-person rating (30A) the 375 kg rated load is 4.3 times the 87.9 kg hull, against R2's scaffold target of 6 times hull mass. The hull is above the carry target, so the ratio cannot reach 6 on this build path.

## Options considered

A: restate R2 as an absolute payload with the ratio reported, not a pass mark. B: keep the 6 times target as unmet and revisit at TRL 4. C: rating changes (see `docs/REVIEW.md`). Recommendation was A.

## Decision

R2 is restated as "payload 375 kg (five persons) within the R3 draft limit; the payload-to-hull ratio is reported". Result: met on paper, 375 kg at 148 mm draft; ratio 4.3 at the rated load (4.6 at 401 kg and 150 mm), reported.

## Consequences

Requirements text, calculation note wording and registers only. No geometry, bill of materials or cost change; value-engineering figures in the register are unchanged.
